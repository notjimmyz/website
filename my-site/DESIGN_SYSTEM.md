# Design Direction

## Soft Editorial Minimalism with Playful Nostalgia

The site should feel:
- warm
- soft
- minimal
- personal
- playful
- slightly nostalgic

Visual language:
- warm cream backgrounds
- soft black text
- restrained pastel accents
- generous whitespace
- strong typography
- simple editorial layouts

Personality can come from occasional:
- pixel-art characters
- cartoon illustrations
- hand-drawn details
- playful microinteractions

Keep the interface ~90% minimal and ~10% playful.

Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics, excessive cards, gradients,
glassmorphism, excessive rounding, and visual clutter.

When in doubt, remove rather than add.
